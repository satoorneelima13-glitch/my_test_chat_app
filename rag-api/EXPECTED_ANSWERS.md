# Expected answers from Apple’s 2025 Form 10-K

These checks are taken from the supplied `_10-K-2025-As-Filed.pdf` and are useful for manually testing the RAG application after ingestion.

## 1. Can you tell me Apple revenue by products and services?

For fiscal 2025, Apple reported the following net sales (dollars in millions):

| Category | 2025 net sales | In billions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| iPhone | $209,586 million | $209.586B |
| Mac | $33,708 million | $33.708B |
| iPad | $28,023 million | $28.023B |
| Wearables, Home and Accessories | $35,686 million | $35.686B |
| Services | $109,158 million | $109.158B |
| **Total net sales** | **$416,161 million** | **$416.161B** |

Source: PDF page 26, printed Form 10-K page 23, “Products and Services Performance.”

## 2. Where is Apple headquarters?

Apple’s headquarters is at **One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, California 95014**.

Sources: PDF page 1 (principal executive offices) and PDF page 20 (headquarters city).
